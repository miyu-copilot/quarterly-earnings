SpaceX Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
•Delivered revenue of $14.6 billion, up 161% year-over-year, fueled by our Super Intelligence (SI) segment and
accelerated growth in Connectivity
•Achieved income from operations of $2.8 billion, net income of $4.4 billion, and Adjusted EBITDA of $8.8
billion
•Successfully launched our first revenue-generating Starship mission, delivering 26 Starlink V3 satellites to our
broadband constellation, representing approximately 10x the downlink capacity added by a Falcon 9 Starlink
launch
•Continued to scale nameplate compute, reaching 1.7 GW as of September 30, 2026
•Added $6.7 billion of incremental SI solutions and infrastructure revenue year-over-year, driven by cloud services
agreements, the acquisition of Cursor, and our growing SI models and software applications, including Grok Bot
•Delivered year-over-year revenue growth of 69% and income from operations growth of 99% for the Connectivity
segment, supported by strong momentum in enterprise and government adoption, and continued Starlink
consumer subscriber growth
SpaceX (Nasdaq: SPCX) announced the following financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, as
compared to the prior year quarter:
•Revenues of $14.6 billion, up 161% from $5.6 billion
•Net income of $4.4 billion, an improvement of $5.5 billion from net loss of $1.1 billion
•Adjusted EBITDA of $8.8 billion, up 306% from $2.2 billion
CFO Commentary:
We delivered another strong quarter in Q3. Revenue grew an impressive 161% year-over-year, fueled by continued
momentum in Connectivity and a clear inflection in our Super Intelligence business. The combination of our launch
leadership, recurring Starlink revenue, and the significant expansion of our SI infrastructure continues to drive meaningful
operating leverage.
We are building the infrastructure of the future with discipline — focused on capital efficiency, strong returns on invested
capital, and maintaining a robust, investment-grade balance sheet — while moving with the velocity to capture the
opportunity in front of us.
Note: Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. See Note 1 for additional information regarding Adjusted EBITDA. A reconciliation to the most
comparable GAAP financial measure is provided under “Reconciliation of Net Income (Loss) to Adjusted EBITDA.”

Space Business Highlights
•Space revenues were $1.8 billion, up 84% sequentially and 6% year-over-year. We completed 12 customer launches during
the quarter, including NASA’s mission to launch the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope
•Completed Starship Flight 14, our first orbital Starship flight, delivering 26 Starlink V3 satellites to our broadband
constellation, representing approximately 10x the downlink capacity added by a Falcon 9 Starlink launch

Launch missions, three commercial Starfall missions, as well as a contract to deliver a payload to the lunar surface with
Starship
•Announced the planned development of Starbase, Louisiana, our next-generation spaceport designed to support thousands of
Starship flights a year, with the first launch targeted for 2029
•Total costs and expenses for the Space segment were up $367 million year-over-year as we continued to accelerate R&D
investments in our Starship program

cloud services agreements, the acquisition of Cursor, and increased Grok adoption across enterprise customers and consumers

Other Key Business Developments
Cursor Merger
SpaceX completed its acquisition of Cursor on August 14, 2026, based on an implied equity value of $60 billion, advancing the
Company’s strategy to vertically integrate compute infrastructure, SI models, and applications.
Under the terms of the agreement, certain outstanding common shares of Cursor were converted into SpaceX equity awards with time-
based vesting conditions to retain key talent. In addition, any outstanding, unvested equity awards held by Cursor employees prior to
the acquisition were assumed and converted into equivalent SpaceX awards. The GAAP purchase consideration was $42.5 billion, and
the value of equity awards issued in connection with the acquisition attributable to post-acquisition service is $23.4 billion, subject to
future forfeitures.
From the closing date through September 30, 2026, Cursor contributed $608 million of revenue to the Company’s consolidated results
of operations.
800 MHz Spectrum
On October 8, 2026, SpaceX entered into an agreement to acquire a nationwide low-band spectrum license portfolio of up to 14
megahertz of paired spectrum in the 800 MHz band. The transaction is expected to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory
and other customary closing conditions.

Note 1. Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Management believes that certain financial measures that are not presented in accordance with GAAP provide management and
investors with useful supplemental information that provides a meaningful view of our financial condition and results of operations
across periods by removing the impact of items that management believes do not directly reflect our ongoing operating performance.
Adjusted EBITDA and Segment Adjusted EBITDA are supplemental measures that are not required by or presented in accordance
with GAAP. In evaluating our performance as measured by Adjusted EBITDA and Segment Adjusted EBITDA, management
recognizes and considers the limitations of these measures. Other companies in our industry may calculate Adjusted EBITDA and
Segment Adjusted EBITDA differently than we do or may not calculate them at all, limiting their usefulness as comparative measures.
Because of these limitations, Adjusted EBITDA and Segment Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as a
substitute for net income (loss), income (loss) from operations, or any other measure calculated in accordance with GAAP, and should
be considered together with our GAAP financial measures and the reconciliations to the corresponding most directly comparable
GAAP financial measures set forth under “Reconciliation of Net Income (Loss) to Adjusted EBITDA” and elsewhere in this release.
Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) excluding (i) depreciation and amortization, (ii) share-based compensation, (iii)
share-based compensation related to equity awards granted in connection with our acquisitions, (iv) acquisition-related costs which
include direct transaction costs related to legal, regulatory, and other professional services fees, (v) restructuring charges, (vi)
impairments, (vii) interest expense, (viii) interest income, (ix) other income (expense), net and (x) provision for (benefit from) income
taxes.
Segment Adjusted EBITDA is defined as segment income (loss) from operations excluding (i) depreciation and amortization, (ii)
share-based compensation, (iii) share-based compensation related to equity awards granted in connection with our acquisitions, (iv)
8
acquisition-related costs which include direct transaction costs related to legal, regulatory, and other professional services fees, (v)
restructuring charges, and (vi) impairments.
Adjusted EBITDA and Segment Adjusted EBITDA are key performance measures that our management uses to assess our financial
performance as well as for internal planning and forecasting purposes. We consider Adjusted EBITDA and Segment Adjusted
EBITDA to be meaningful performance measures for investors to evaluate our operating performance and to compare the financial
results between periods.
Note 2. Other Definitions
Super Intelligence (SI) refers to the operating and reportable segment previously reported as Artificial Intelligence (AI).
Starlink Consumer Subscribers refers to the metric previously disclosed as Starlink Subscribers. We use this measure to assess the
adoption of Starlink as we expand within and across geographies and business segments. Starlink consumer subscribers includes both
personal (e.g., residential and roam) and business (e.g., local priority and global priority) subscription plans, but does not include
managed enterprise and government customers with contracts in domains including aviation, maritime, land mobility, fixed sites, and
government entities.
Total Satellites in Orbit includes both operational and de-orbit approved satellites. Our prior Satellites in Orbit definition reflected
operational satellites only and would have excluded approximately 400 satellites from our Q3 2026 total. This definitional change has
no impact on our financial results.
